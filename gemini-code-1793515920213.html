About me

I’m Rakshit, a product enthusiast with a Computer Science background. What pulls me in is building products that solve real problems.

My road into product management started with building things. I made my own products, then worked on features at Predis.ai, and what I enjoyed most was working out why users do what they do, finding where they get stuck, and changing the product based on what I learned.

At Predis.ai I did user research, worked on the Reference Image feature from start to finish, studied how people used the product and tested changes. One test, a nudge inside the product, brought more users back: retention was 23% higher than in the group that didn’t see it.

Outside work I built TryApprove and BlurTab, which taught me what it takes to get an idea to launch, find users and learn from what they say. TryApprove has 100+ users and has brought in $235 in revenue. BlurTab has 50+ users, all from organic channels. Both are on the side projects page.

I’m doing my PGDM at Welingkar in Mumbai to understand the business side of technology better.

I like connecting what users need, what the business wants and what the technology can do, and then making a call. The areas I find most interesting are SaaS, AI products and consumer tech.

I think the hard part of product work is deciding what is worth building, more than building it.

Work

Predis.ai

Product Management Intern, Nov 2025 to May 2026

Identified where new users dropped off, then tested the fix

Predis.ai uses AI to generate social media content. My focus was on improving the new-user experience and bringing features from idea to launch.

  • I led the discovery and launch of the Reference Image feature, letting users recreate ads using Predis.ai’s catalog or their own uploads.
  • I talked with users to understand what they needed, mapped out the steps they'd take, and worked closely with design and engineering to bring the feature to life.
  • By digging into Mixpanel and SQL data, I found exactly where new users were dropping off and identified clear ways to make the experience smoother.
  • I designed and tested a subtle nudge inside the app to encourage users to stick around. The group that saw it had a 23% higher retention rate.
  • I also kept an eye on what competitors were doing, sketched out early wireframes, and relied on real user feedback to keep making the product better.

Side projects

Two of these have real users, and one has paying customers.

TryApprove

A client approval and feedback portal, built and run by me, ongoing

One portal for client approvals, feedback and invoices

Agencies and freelancers spend a lot of effort getting approvals and feedback out of clients. TryApprove keeps all of it in one portal under the agency’s own branding, from onboarding to the final invoice.

  • I started by talking to agency owners and freelancers about how approvals work for them today.
  • Search traffic, Instagram and cold outreach brought in 100+ users, and their feedback went straight back into the product.
  • Some of those early users paid: $235 in revenue so far, and $78 a month in recurring revenue at the first version.

BlurTab

Privacy browser extension

Hiding sensitive details while you share your screen

Screen sharing has a habit of showing things you never meant to show. I spotted the problem during a share, so I built a browser extension that hides sensitive information on the screen.

  • I launched it through organic channels only and got 50+ users, which was enough to tell me the problem was real.
  • Feedback from those users pointed at usability gaps, and I fixed them over several rounds.
